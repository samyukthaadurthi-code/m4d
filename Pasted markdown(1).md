## Initial Location Introductions

### Madurai

Madurai is the commercial and cultural centre of Southern Tamil Nadu. Its established economy, expanding urban footprint and connections to surrounding districts make it a natural starting point for MRC Landmarks.

### Dindigul

Dindigul benefits from its position between several major Tamil Nadu cities and its connections to agricultural, manufacturing, educational and tourism economies.

### Theni

Set near the Western Ghats, Theni combines a strong agricultural economy with connections to Madurai and Kerala. Its local character and surrounding landscape make it distinct from larger urban markets.

### Tenkasi

Tenkasi is a growing district centre with strong regional identity, tourism activity and connections to Tirunelveli and neighbouring areas.

### Sivakasi

Sivakasi is one of Southern Tamil Nadu’s best-known industrial centres. Its manufacturing base and surrounding business communities contribute to consistent local housing and land requirements.

### Ramanathapuram

Ramanathapuram connects coastal communities, tourism, trade and culturally important destinations. MRC is studying opportunities in the wider district as part of its long-term regional vision.

These are introductory narratives, not investment claims. Expand them only with verified research and confirmed project context.

## Forum Introduction

## What MRC Forum Is

MRC Forum is a membership-based real-estate business network created for Southern Tamil Nadu.

It brings together professionals from complementary categories and organises them through local city chapters. Members can participate in structured meetings, business introductions, project conversations, knowledge sessions and regional networking opportunities.

MRC Landmarks provides the platform and direction. The strength of the Forum comes from the quality, participation and credibility of its members.

## Channel Partner or Forum Member?

These are two different relationships.

A channel partner is authorised to represent and sell eligible MRC Landmarks projects. The relationship is focused on project sales, buyer communication and sales support.

A Forum member joins the wider MRC professional network to build relationships, discover collaboration opportunities and connect with businesses across the real-estate ecosystem.

**Best suited for:** Brokers, developers, architects, contractors, designers, suppliers, legal professionals, financial advisers and related businesses.

**CTA:** Apply for Forum Membership

## MRC Forum FAQs

### What is MRC Forum?

MRC Forum is a professional business network connecting people and companies across the Southern Tamil Nadu real-estate ecosystem.

### Is MRC Forum only for property brokers?

No. It includes developers, architects, contractors, designers, suppliers, professional advisers and other businesses connected to real estate.

### Is a Forum member automatically an MRC Channel Partner?

No. Forum membership and channel partnership are separate. A Forum member interested in selling MRC projects must complete the channel-partner approval process.

### Can I apply from any city?

Applications can be submitted from across Southern Tamil Nadu. Chapter availability and participation options depend on the Forum’s active city network.

### Is membership automatically approved?

No. Applications are reviewed to maintain relevance, professional standards and a useful balance of member categories.

### Is there a membership fee?

Membership fees and inclusions will be shared according to the approved membership category. **[CLIENT TO CONFIRM final policy]**

### Does membership guarantee leads or business?

No. The Forum creates opportunities to connect, participate and build relationships. It does not guarantee leads, referrals, transactions or revenue.
