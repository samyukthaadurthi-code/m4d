What happens at the Forum

Meet. Match. Learn.

  1. Meet

    One evening a month in Madurai to begin with, then Trichy and Coimbatore. Small rooms, real conversations, no stage.

  2. Match

    Developers looking for brokers who know a locality. Brokers looking for verified stock. Architects and advisers looking for both. We make the introductions.

  3. Learn

    Short sessions on what actually moves a deal — DTCP and RERA paperwork, reading a layout, bank-loan eligibility, the questions buyers ask.

What members get

First look, first call.

  • An early look at MRC projects before they go public, with pricing and plot maps.
  • A verified member directory — name, firm and the areas each member works.
  • Priority invitations to site walks, launch events and partner briefings.
  • A moderated WhatsApp circle: introductions and opportunities, not forwards.
