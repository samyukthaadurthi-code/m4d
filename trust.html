Home  /  Trust & Transparency

Clarity at Every Step.

Trust is built through information that can be checked — not broad promises. Every project page displays the approvals and documentation that apply to it.

DTCP Approved

Town planning approval

The layout has been approved by the Directorate of Town and Country Planning, which governs how land may be divided, where roads run and what space is reserved for shared use.

RERA Registered

Regulatory compliance

The project is registered with the state authority, which requires the developer to publish project details and adhere to declared timelines.

Title Verified

Legal documentation

Ownership has been traced through its documentation so the seller's right to transfer the land can be established.

Bank Approved

Loan eligible

Lenders have reviewed the project, which means buyers can pursue financing rather than funding a purchase entirely themselves.

Survey Completed

Boundary verified

Plot boundaries have been measured and marked on the ground, so what is shown on the layout matches what exists at the site.

Infrastructure

Status tracked

Roads, drainage, water, electricity and street lighting are recorded by their actual stage of completion rather than described in general terms.
