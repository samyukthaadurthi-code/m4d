Who we are

A Madurai land company, rooted in the South.

MRC began in Madurai, in a family that has held and farmed land around the city for two generations. We plan, document and deliver land that families can build a future on — approved, surveyed and honest.

Our focus is Southern Tamil Nadu: Madurai first, then the districts we know — Dindigul, Virudhunagar, Sivaganga and Theni.
