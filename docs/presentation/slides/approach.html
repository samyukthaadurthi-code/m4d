Land to Landmark: six things checked before a plot is offered.

DTCP approved

Town-planning approval governs how the land is divided, where roads run and what is reserved for shared use.

TNRERA registered

Registered with the state authority, which requires published project details and declared timelines.

Title verified

Ownership traced through its documents, so the right to transfer the land is established.

Bank approved

Lenders have reviewed the layout, so buyers can take a home loan rather than fund the whole purchase.

Survey completed

Plot boundaries measured and marked on the ground with stones — the drawing matches the site.

Infrastructure tracked

Roads, drainage, water, electricity and lighting recorded by their real stage of completion.
