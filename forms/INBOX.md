# The sales inbox

**https://forms.mrclandmarks.com/inbox**

Everything anyone sends to the MRC WhatsApp number (95148 89555), and a box to
reply from. Written for whoever is on sales, not for an engineer.

## Signing in

One passcode for the whole team. Ask Sudharsan for it. You stay signed in for a
month, so you will rarely type it.

Keep the page open on a phone or a laptop. The tab title shows how many people
are waiting, so you can see it without looking at the page.

## Reading and replying

Conversations are listed newest first. A teal number means unread. Tap one to
read the whole thing.

Three voices appear in a conversation:

| Looks like | Who |
|---|---|
| White bubble, on the left | The customer |
| Pale blue bubble, on the right | Thamarai, the assistant |
| Solid teal bubble, on the right | You |

Type in the box at the bottom and press Send. That is it — it goes to their
WhatsApp from the MRC number.

## Thamarai stops when you start

Thamarai answers everything by herself until you send a message in that
conversation. From then on she stays out of it, so the customer never gets two
different answers, and a green bar at the top says **"You are handling this."**

When you are finished, tap **"Give it back to Thamarai"** and she picks it up
again. If you forget, she takes it back by herself after a day.

## When you cannot type a reply

Sometimes the box is replaced by an orange panel saying you cannot reply.

This is a WhatsApp rule, not a fault. WhatsApp only lets a business type a free
reply **within 24 hours of the customer's own last message**. After that the
only thing allowed is a pre-approved template.

Call them instead — there is a Call button right there. The moment they message
again the box comes back.

## Adding a new template

Templates are the messages we are allowed to send outside those 24 hours
(the badge, the site-visit confirmation, the thank-you after an event). They are
written in Meta's own tool, not here:

**business.facebook.com → WhatsApp Manager → Message templates**
(there is a link at the bottom of the conversation list)

Two things to know, because they cause almost every rejection:

- Pick the right category. Anything that reads like promotion must be
  **Marketing**, not Utility. Submitting a promotional message as Utility is
  rejected every time.
- Do not start or end the message with a `{{1}}` placeholder.

Approval takes anywhere from a few minutes to two days. A new template can only
be *sent* from the website code, so tell Sudharsan once it is approved.

## Limits worth knowing

- We may start a conversation with **250 new people in any 24 hours**. Replying
  to someone who wrote to us first does not count towards that and is free.
- A reply inside the 24-hour window costs nothing. Starting a conversation with
  a template costs roughly ₹1.

## When something looks wrong

Nothing here deletes anything — every message is kept in the spreadsheet under
the **WA_Messages** tab, so nothing is ever lost, even if the page misbehaves.
Call Sudharsan.
